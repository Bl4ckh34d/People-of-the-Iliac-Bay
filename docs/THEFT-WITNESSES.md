Approved requirement, 14 October 2026: taking goods from shop shelves or private property in houses and other buildings succeeds with 100% probability when no living NPC or guard is nearby and able to witness the act. Dead NPCs never count. Merely belonging to the building, being upstairs behind an opaque floor, or existing in the population ledger does not make an NPC a witness. Use actual nearby position and unobstructed sight to the theft/player. Retain the applicable skill-based detection check when a living potential witness exists; do not replace witnessed theft with automatic success or automatic failure.

Sleeping residents must follow the sleep/awareness system: do not treat an unaware sleeper as an awake eyewitness. They may become witnesses if the act wakes/alerts them. This integrates with the planned distance/skill detection model and must be tested explicitly.
