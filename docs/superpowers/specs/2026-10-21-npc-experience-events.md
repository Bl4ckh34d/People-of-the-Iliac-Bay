This catalog defines cases and evidence boundaries; it does not claim these relationship effects or adapters are implemented. ## Event contract

Treat an event as an observed change in the world, not as a line of dialogue. Keep authoritative native facts separate from each NPC's own observations, attributed reports, uncertain beliefs and inferences. A model may appraise a bounded event projection, but it must not receive hidden facts, invent evidence, identify an unknown culprit, or convert a claim into world truth. Social appraisal uses only facts available to that NPC at the captured decision revision. An NPC who learns an event later receives a separate, attributed knowledge event.

Persist one stable event or receipt ID, game-time occurrence, participants, source, native outcome, affected obligation, and observer-specific knowledge. Apply each permitted relationship dimension once per recipient and event, even when dialogue, memory, a task receipt, a summary, or a second subsystem surfaces the same outcome. Relationship direction is explicit: A helping B can affect B toward A; it does not update A toward B unless A has evidence that matters to A. Witnesses receive effects only for what they perceive or later learn through a recorded path.

Effects below are examples for policy design, not fixed deltas or universal moral ratings. Mood, affinity, trust, fear, resentment, loyalty and obligation remain distinct. An event can change one, several, or none. A refusal is not an insult; a purchase at agreed terms is expected exchange, not generosity; a promise is not fulfillment. Conditional interpretation must not use semantic keyword rules or a new model call on every hit, frame, or status tick.

Mood cools only toward neutral with elapsed game time. Use stable per-person variation; a read, prompt, save load, chat reopen, or UI projection must not reroll it. Cooling does not decay affinity, trust, fear, resentment, or loyalty. Main schema-33 work adds temporal mood revision invalidation: each accepted native mood event must advance the mood revision used by readers; chat sequence alone cannot stand in for a world-state revision. Keep the existing appointment no-show penalty and memory receipt in one causal path so the future shared appraisal cannot apply the same penalty twice.

Personality remains persistent and separate from current mood and relationship. Preserve exact seeded outputs for the original warmth, patience, curiosity, privacy, and manner axes. Add outlook, emotional stability, ambition, integrity, courage, dutifulness, vocabulary, independent eloquence, and status attitude as stable persisted axes; initialize once, never on a read. Eloquence is independent of politeness and honesty. Noble or educated background can shift the likelihood of eloquence but does not guarantee it, deference, or honesty; a person may be eloquent and manipulative, condescending, or vulgar. Verify the player's actual rank before applying status attitude; do not assume the player is a commoner. Authored quest personas take precedence over generated profiles.

Personality may modulate the semantic appraisal of an event's impact and also the rate at which mood recovers over game time. It is an interpretation factor, never evidence: humor does not erase an actual insult, sensitivity does not prove hostile intent, and emotional stability does not change the receipt or outcome. Context, relationship and relevant prior events must be considered alongside traits. Do not reduce this interaction to one universal scalar. The current v2 profile has 17 stable axes, including the added Humor, SelfEsteem and Sensitivity axes; deterministic v1 migration preserves all 14 existing values while deriving the new axes from saved identity and seed. The new Core mood-recovery policy multiplies the stable world-seed/person half-life by bounded, deterministic factors from EmotionalStability, Sensitivity, SelfEsteem and Humor (combined clamp 0.70–1.40); runtime/store caller wiring remains pending. These provisional factors affect only mood cooling toward neutral; they do not appraise evidence or alter relationship dimensions. Never reroll traits or recovery factors on reads, saves, or conversations. Event-impact modulation remains a separate semantic appraisal policy.

Acceptance fixtures must compare the same receipt, words, context, relationship and history under two stored profiles that differ only in a relevant stable trait, and show the intended bounded difference in mood/relationship appraisal. Separate paired fixtures vary relationship, event context or history while holding traits fixed. A humor trait may affect a clearly contextual joke, but repeated, independently receipted targeted abuse must not be reclassified as joking on that basis alone. Quoted, hypothetical, reported and ambiguous speech must remain distinct from direct speech. Qwen may semantically interpret the receipt-bound utterance and factors; native policy validates identities, evidence, permitted targets, bounds and exactly-once effects. Do not add keyword lists or regex rules, and do not add a new model call for each hit, frame or status tick.

## Implementation boundaries

Current native seams include `Appointments.cs` and appointment no-show memory; `ConversationAttitudes.cs` and conversation mood; `NpcInventory.cs` wallet, exact item ownership, revisions, and atomic transactions; `NpcShopCommerce.cs` native stock pricing/purchase validation; `NpcTasks.cs` and `NpcTaskStore.cs` persisted task phases and outcome/failure receipts; and existing crime, witness, household, death, and schedule systems. These seams provide facts or effects. They do not by themselves implement shared appraisal, generalized directed trust/fear/resentment/loyalty, NPC needs, employment wages, loan obligations, or autonomous economy decisions.

For each future event adapter, capture the native outcome, event ID, game time, participant IDs, observer knowledge, source and revision before appraisal. Reject stale or duplicate events. Validate any appraisal fields and referenced evidence natively. Commit permitted relationship changes with the event's exactly-once ledger and correct save branch. Readers use mood revision for temporal mood changes; chat sequence tracks dialogue only. Existing no-show consequence remains owned by its existing receipt until a migration explicitly makes the shared event ledger its single owner.
