# Shared murder investigation and suspect evidence

## Required behavior

The guard removes the body, then questions witnesses and searches during the fixed case window. Witnesses identify a murderer only if they actually witnessed the crime. Identity is separate from a last observed location. A guard must actually see the suspect before arresting the player or fighting an NPC. A dead NPC murderer has their exact corpse cleared and the guards return to duty.

Searching guards may contact available exterior guards within 35 metres (the user's suggested 25–50 metre range). Recruits may relay the case on later updates. All participants share one case and the original remaining deadline. Maximum six total participants per case, including the first responder. Contact and recruitment cannot renew the deadline, reveal an unseen suspect position, start combat remotely, or create new guards.

## Integration work

1. Add shared-case recruitment to the existing roster/report ownership model. `GuardReport.Id` and `GuardRecord.IncidentId` already identify the case; `GuardIds` supplies the aggregate cap. Reject unavailable, dead, indoor, refuge, late and seventh candidates. Validate real routes and preserve return-to-post geometry atomically before publishing membership.
2. Preserve the investigation entrance/path invariants. Existing investigation routes must end at the case entrance and searching positions are evaluated on the saved case patrol. A route to a recruiter therefore needs a surveyed continuation to that search anchor, or an explicit saved join phase; merely accepting a different endpoint would teleport the recruit onto the patrol. No immediate `BeginCombat` on recruitment.
3. Replace the current separate `pursuit:<guard>:<time>` recruitment reports for these cases. Native contact uses actual loaded actors, distance and sight/hearing checks, with a caller snapshot to prevent same-frame townwide cascading. The old player-crime-only gate must not suppress an investigation with an unknown murderer.
4. Add typed suspect identity and witnessed-identity provenance to the case. Reuse existing stable player/resident/native-enemy identities. Keep a verified sighting record separate, including observer, actual position and time. The existing runtime assignment of `ThreatId` on an investigation violates its current saved-report contract and must be replaced rather than weakening validation globally.
5. Integrate mobile and static witness contacts into the same case; independent `witness:` reports must not bypass the six-guard total. Preserve genuine direct observation of an ongoing crime as independent native evidence. General resident suspects need their own real combat adapter; the current enemy adapter supports registered hostile `EnemyEntity` instances only. Never fall back to accusing the player when the NPC suspect cannot be resolved.
6. On a real sighting, issue Halt, notify nearby guards through the bounded case rules, and use native arrest/player combat or the exact NPC target. An identity alone never calls player `BeginCombat`. Expired search and resolved/dead-suspect cleanup return each participant physically. Genuine ongoing combat must remain distinct from the fixed witness-search window.
