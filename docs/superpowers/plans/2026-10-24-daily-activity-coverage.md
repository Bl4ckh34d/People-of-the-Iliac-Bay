# Daily activity coverage implementation plan

**Goal:** Restore useful daytime and evening activity through valid persistent routines, real destinations and actual Inn behavior.

**Architecture:** Extend the existing routine, journey and composed-schedule infrastructure. Preserve active visits, appointments, health/danger ownership, native roles and stable identities. Do not replace the population or teleport actors to meet an occupancy target.

**Evidence:** [Daggerfall audit](../../2026-10-24-daggerfall-daytime-population-audit.md). The user has prioritized this repair within the already authorized full mod implementation.

## User clarification: activity kinds and dayparts

Ordinary locals should fill the day with plausible shops, guilds, cemetery, castle and other public-place activities, including intermediate home stops. Expand the available daily goal budget to roughly five times the current size; available time, needs, habits, actual resources and native duties still determine execution. This does not authorize a mandatory sequence of repetitive temple or shopping visits.

Morning Inn traffic is primarily travelers arriving after native gate opening, checking in and leaving to pursue city activities before returning for the evening. Keep the traveler's actual booking and paid lodging separate from a local's visit. Local social drinking starts in the late afternoon/evening and can last late. A midday Inn meal is a separate purpose, eligible when going home would consume too much of the meal break. This updates the older absolute home-first rule: prefer home when reasonably close, but distance/time can justify a closer Inn even when home is technically reachable. Food and drink still require actual purchase/consumption receipts.

Daggerfall's active haunting requires every resident outing to return home before the danger window. Once the quest genuinely ends, later eligible evening planning follows ordinary town rules. Preserve observed travel and completed activities when changing that policy; do not rewrite earlier day history or teleport the actor.

## Persistent multi-outing integration

### Family and social coordination

Use actual `PartnerId`, `MotherId` and `FatherId`, not merely two adults sharing an address. For households with children, optional outings should normally alternate so a parent remains home when feasible. Essential work, appointments, danger and urgent health retain their existing ownership rules. Approximately 10% of eligible shared leisure opportunities can send both parents out together; choose this deterministically from the household/day/activity identity so save/reload cannot reroll it. A home containing only children remains locked.

Shared leisure belongs predominantly in late afternoon/evening. Partners or known friends can arrange an evening Inn meeting and arrive separately. A meeting requires actual NPC participants and their consent/availability; the current player-plus-one-NPC appointment record must not be duplicated to fake an NPC-to-NPC event. Publish participant plans together or neither. Keep shared activity identity, waiting, cancellation, actual attendance and the later split/return distinct.

Shared walking must use resident collision-checked movement, the slower member's actual pace and a progress clock that stops when either member waits or talks. The guard patrol's absolute-time positions and direct transform placement are not suitable unchanged. Preserve actual member positions across interruption/reload and release each member once onto an individual continuation when they split.

For shared Inn seating, add real table identity and an atomic pair of distinct nearby seats with compatible facing. Existing per-seat leases are individual and currently insufficient. Both actual purchases retain separate wallets/receipts. Conversation and joint departure require actual attendance; planned visits alone do not create conversation memories. A pair may walk home together after meeting; different homes allow a shared section followed by individual returns. Preserve separate local lunch, social-drink and traveler room-booking purposes.

Read-only Luna/Max checks identified the concrete seams: family fields and fixed appointment conflict handling; `DailySchedule.OutingsDay`; resident `CrowdMotion`/arrival continuity; exact furniture seat IDs and native counter transactions. Missing shared invitation identity, household publication, paused group motion, table grouping and pair-seat claims remain implementation work, not completed features.

Reuse `DailySchedule.OutingsDay` and `ResidentVisitJourney`. Persist up to five non-overlapping home-return outings, with at most 25 visits and an explicit total route-point bound. Legacy version-1 routines map to their one existing outing without inventing new history. Use one canonical copy/validation helper across save, dialogue, route migration, appointments, observed visits and recovery. Every visit needs a stable outing/stop identity so completion of one cannot replay or satisfy another. Point activities such as native surveyed cemetery or market places require their own real provider and route evidence; a building entry alone does not prove these places exist.

Rank up to 20–30 candidate venues, then survey selected chains rather than a complete graph. Five chains of five visits need at most 30 initial native legs (home to first, each consecutive visit, final return), plus a bounded fallback allowance. Share planning and path-search budgets across the whole resident day. Each outing uses its own feasible daypart interval; do not apply the old three-hour away minimum separately to all five outings. A failed path means alternate verified goals or home, never invented movement.
