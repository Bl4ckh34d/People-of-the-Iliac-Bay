# Superseded sleeping plan

This plan is historical. Latest rule: one NPC per bed, household capacity equals actual beds, no independent five-person cap. Shared sleepers and modular heads/blankets are rejected. Further art is deferred; continue citizen and guard behavior per docs/CIVILIAN-RESPONSES.md.

# Sleeping implementation plan

Use executing-plans and test-driven-development for this approved milestone. Use imagegen for generated sprite art and requesting-code-review before the implementation checkpoint.

**Goal:** Make residents visibly sleep at real beds, retain identity when waking, and extend this to original NPCs and paid tavern occupants.

**Architecture:** Read stable bed instances and their mattress planes from original interior records and model geometry. Keep bed assignment separate from household size: parents and children may share, with deterministic distinct logical slots. Directional blanket/face art is anchored to the bed and selected for the camera view; a pose renderer changes visuals without replacing resident, service or quest identity. Logical sharing slots do not prove that individual full-body sprites physically fit. Tavern reservation rights remain a separate persisted booking policy and must govern traveler/player occupancy.

**Tech stack:** DFU 1.1.1, Unity 2019.4.40f1, C# 7.3/netstandard2.0 core, net8 behavior tests, standard mod assets.

**Spec:** `docs/MILESTONES.md`, `DIRECTIONAL-CIVILIAN-ANIMATION.md`, `TAVERN-LODGING.md`, `QUEST-IDENTITY.md` and the approved living-cities design.

## Constraints

- Up to five per generated family; beds are soft demographic preferences, never a hard one-person-per-bed cap.
- Sleeping sprites for all NPC flats come before directional movement/combat production. Begin with one fully tested identity-matched pose, then expand the inventory; a pilot does not complete all-appearance coverage.
- No generic appearance substitution, rotated standing sprites or cloned quest identities as the finished behavior.
- Preserve original quest components, hidden state and activation identity. Cross-building service moves need separate binding tests.
- A tavern may be fully booked; no guaranteed player bed. Do not assign residents to guest rooms without booking authority.
- Unknown/modded geometry must be reported as unsupported until validated; never put a sleeper in guessed furniture or through a wall.
