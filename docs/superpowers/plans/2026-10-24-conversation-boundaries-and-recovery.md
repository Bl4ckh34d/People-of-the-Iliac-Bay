# Conversation boundaries, recovery, and relevant speech

## Requirements

Updated user requirements supersede the original broad reporting policy: player insults and unwanted advances end conversation but never justify a guard report by themselves. An initial offense permits angry re-entry. Another actual offense causes a personality-dependent multi-day contact cooldown. After expiry, cautious conversation and apology are possible while resentment persists. Actual threats prevent further contact; only severe/credible threats justify a receipt-bound complaint. A threat-only uncorroborated case needs easier native defense and a small fine instead of prison. An explanation remains a claim, not verified truth or restored friendship.

Schema-63 source corrections pass 1781/1781 Core, 12/12 persisted-boundary and 65 speech-policy/ledger checks. These supersede the broad escalation and unconditional inherited-refusal policy described in the historical schema-60 implementation below. Parent refusal evidence is validated during staging and restore. Unbound legacy complaints retire without dispatch and the legacy Trespassing adapter is disabled. The revised native probe compiles but has not run. Threat credibility, court handling and native acceptance remain unfinished. Installed package remains schema 61.

Anger, resentment and fear can rise quickly after a serious event. They recover slowly with game time and, even with the clock paused, through many accepted safe conversation turns. A failed generation, replay or hostile turn does not earn recovery. Saved role policy still protects nobles, guards and experienced combatants. Weather remains available as verified context without becoming a default conversational topic.

## Implemented source

- Every foreground routed turn receives exact-source speech classification before task or offer dispatch. This closes the neutral-action route that previously converted sexual violence threats into generic task refusals.
- Schema 60 stores the latest boundary, exact issued receipt and ordinal, repair highwater, parent refusal provenance and report state. Commit stages mood at most -40 and one relationship event with resentment at least 65. Direct threats can raise civilian fear to 65; protected ranks and combat roles receive no fear increase from this path.
- Mood and directed emotions retain their saved personality-aware game-time recovery anchors. Safe accepted speech appraisals cool fear and resentment by 1 for a zero model proposal, or at most 2 for an explicit negative proposal. Negative mood after a saved boundary gains at most 2 per accepted positive social turn. Replays, failures, hostile speech and active refusal receive no automatic cooling.
- The exact foreground boundary receipt is consumed by later background appraisal without applying the incident twice.
- Recovery checks the exact issued speech ordinal against the latest boundary. An older queued greeting cannot calm newer fear or resentment merely because its appraisal runs later. The parent regression failed at 65 versus 63 before the barrier, then passed after the fix, including restore and the paused clock.
- A boundary withdraws only source-bound player-requested tasks/actions through the existing cancellation/routine-return path. Autonomous health recovery and institutional duties retain their admission rules. Native movement acceptance remains open.
- Reentry supplies the saved refusal to both classification and speech generation. Repair reopens communication without resetting saved emotions. Terminal responses append and accept the exact speech before window teardown; no cancelled audio callback owns the incident.
- Guard complaints preserve the original scene and receipt, retry available responders and reach Delivered only after native roster dispatch. Threats use native Assault; abuse uses native Vagrancy because DFU has no verbal-harassment offense. An existing native crime is preserved. Outdoor witnesses physically seek a guard; stationary indoor NPCs use the existing building response path.
- Portrait selection reads the real saved emotional state. A temporary failed live read retains the last valid state for the same binding; changing speaker clears the cache.
- The social prompt treats weather as background, answers weather questions from verified facts and forbids unsupported weather explanations for mood.
