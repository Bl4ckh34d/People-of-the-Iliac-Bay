# Legitimate defense and loot rights

User requirement, 14 October 2026: killing a genuine hostile attacker, such as an attacking burglar, assassin, thief or creature, is not Murder, indoors or outdoors in a settlement. Guards still attend to secure the scene. If the player was seen fighting or killing the attacker, guards approach to ask whether the player is all right, sometimes offer a healing potion, and explain that the defeated attacker's belongings may be taken.

Loot rights apply immediately to a legitimate-defense kill. The player does not have to wait for a guard to arrive or grant verbal permission. Taking those belongings is not Theft, whether observed or not. Save the entitlement with the victim/corpse identity so transitions and reloads preserve it. Do not clear an already-earned entitlement merely because witnesses leave or die.

Existing bounties and other known crimes survive lawful self-defense. When the situational welfare check is available, guards complete that brief exchange, recognize the wanted player and then pursue the applicable outstanding charges through normal surrender/arrest. The courtesy dialogue does not pardon crimes, clear regional reputation/bounty state, grant temporary immunity beyond the exchange, or convert the lawful defense into Murder. If the courtesy window has expired, existing crime enforcement proceeds normally without a delayed welfare exchange. Corpse loot entitlement remains valid even if the player is wanted for an unrelated offense.

Classify the incident from combat provenance: a genuine attacker is distinct from a civilian who turned hostile because the player attacked them, killed a relative or committed another crime. Provoking a civilian into defense must not make killing them lawful or grant loot rights. Background enemy hostility and the initiator of combat must survive the handoff from stationary to mobile actors and save/load. Do not infer a criminal profession from appearance or faction alone.

The welfare dialogue requires a witness who actually saw the fight, including guards with line of sight. Body discovery alone can authorize scene security but cannot invent a witnessed heroic act. Make healing-potion offers occasional, bounded and persistent per incident so repeated activation/reloads cannot farm gifts. Legitimate-defense scenes should not use unidentified-visitor trespass warnings against the player while the player is cooperating with scene security or taking entitled loot.

The user explicitly requires a situational approach within the next few minutes, never hours or days later. Use a five-in-game-minute default expiry from the end of the fight, configurable when the feature is implemented. Leaving the local incident area expires the welfare opportunity sooner. Store the absolute expiry and consumed/expired state so unloading, reloads and returning cannot queue a delayed greeting or potion offer. Normal scene security may continue after this window; earned loot rights do not expire with the courtesy dialogue.

Implementation is pending for the future dangerous-event actors, combat-provenance ledger, welfare dialogue and inventory/loot-rights adapter. The current generated-resident corpse pipeline covers civilian violence and has no loot inventory. Native enemy combat remains outside that generated-resident interception. Acceptance must cover unprovoked attackers, player-provoked civilians, indoor/outdoor scenes, witnessed/unwitnessed defense, immediate looting before guard arrival, innocent passersby, potion limits and save/load.
