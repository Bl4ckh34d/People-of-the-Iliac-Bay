# Paid service and emergency recovery: integration design

> Historical design. The current [Roadmap](ROADMAP.md) defers companion recruitment and limits future payment to simple daily pay. Existing ledger code is not a reason to expand minute-based escrow or refund machinery.

Status: source-based design; no production implementation or gameplay acceptance. This narrows the paid-service gap in [goal arbitration](2026-10-23-goal-arbitration-design.md). Companion contracts are not guard allegiance, quest ownership, or native combat orders.

## Current seam and constraint

`StreetPopulation.HealthCare.PrepareNpcHealthCare` can choose a feasible health task, but `HasActivePaidServiceDuty` turns *any* Active companion contract into `ActiveServiceDuty`; `NpcGoalArbiter.Select` then defers even Emergency recovery. The service ledger has `TrySuspend`, `TryResume`, `TryReplace`, and epoch/branch/revision-bound `TryCommitNpcServiceContracts`, but no suspension reason or owner. `TrySuspend` accrues earnings through the suspension time; `TryResume` does not accrue the gap and does not extend `BlockEndAt`. `TrySettle` needs native earnings/refund receipts. The only current native transfer adapter funds a reservation; no gameplay adapter pays earnings or refunds. `NpcHealthCareRecord` already persists the care episode ID and phase, and the task/action overlay owns movement. These facts rule out interpreting a bare `Suspended` status as permission for recovery to resume someone else's contract.

## Minimal contract-owned claim

Extend the saved contract state with `SuspensionOwnerKind` (`None`, `LegacyUnknown`, `HealthCare`) and `SuspensionOwnerId` (the exact `NpcHealthCareRecord.Id`). Keep the claim on the contract, so a single service-ledger transition commits status and cause together; do not try to commit a separate care-row claim after suspension. Add pure `TrySuspendForHealthCare(expectedRevision, now, careEpisodeId)` and `TryResumeFromHealthCare(expectedRevision, now, careEpisodeId)` contract transitions. Add `WorldStateStore.TrySuspendNpcServiceForHealthCare(contractId, personId, careEpisodeId, expectedContractRevision, now)` and corresponding `TryResumeNpcServiceAfterHealthCare` wrappers. The suspend wrapper requires Active status, a live care row for the same `NpcId` in Seeking/Acting/Blocked, an injured observed native vitals sample, and `now <= BlockEndAt`; it derives `TryReplace`, then commits the ticket with empty native-evidence arrays. The pure transition accrues once through `now` and sets Suspended plus the claim. The resume wrapper requires the exact saved claim, Suspended status, no competing active care task, recovered care or full observed native health, and `now < BlockEndAt`; the pure transition clears the claim and resumes without earning the suspended interval. The existing unowned `TryResume` must reject a health-owned suspension. Other suspension causes remain distinct; health care must neither adopt nor resume `LegacyUnknown` or another owner's suspension.

Version the contract record and world save migration together. A version-1 Active contract gains `None`; a version-1 Suspended contract gains `LegacyUnknown` and remains ineligible for automatic health-care resume. Validate owner kind/status/ID combinations on restore. A Recovered care row can later be replaced by a new episode, so reconciliation must run before replacement; if interrupted by save/load, the contract's old claim still identifies the episode and can be reconciled only from verified native health and task state. No transient dictionary or actor component is the source of suspension ownership.

## Runtime admission and billing

Use the already selected *fresh* treatment task to evaluate the actual contract and its terms, then arbitrate again after any transition. Emergency self-preservation may request a health-owned suspension of ordinary paid service; it does not bypass quest protection, active native combat/alarm ownership, or the one-active-task gate. Important/Urgent policy can remain stricter, but no Active companion contract is an unconditional Emergency veto. A failed or stale suspension leaves the paid service owner intact and admits no care task. On success, re-read the contract, motivation generation, care revision, task, vitals, and treatment target before `TryAcceptAndBindNpcHealthCareTask`; the existing task/action executor remains the sole mover. If task admission fails after suspension, keep the claim for retry/reconciliation, with no billable availability while care has displaced service. On complete native recovery, finish/cancel the care action and save its physical return first, then resume only the matching claim. An external cure uses the same handoff. Do not flip the service to Active solely because a goal is Fulfilled or a care row says Recovered.

`RestTreatment` governs *the chosen rest task*, not all healing. `CountsAsPaidAvailability` with `Rest` coverage permits an Active contract to remain billable during verified bed rest; the service mover must yield to the already accepted care task, and normal `TryAccrue` remains applicable. `SuspendBillingDuringRest` and `RestNotIncluded` require health-owned suspension before rest starts. Owned potion, guild, or shop treatment that interrupts the companion's availability also requires suspension, regardless of rest terms. If an episode starts with paid rest and later switches to another treatment, suspend before that next task. If it starts suspended and later chooses rest, keep the suspension for the episode; do not silently restart billing mid-care. A paid-rest plan that loses its native bed/receipt must fail or replan and cannot be counted as completed availability by assertion alone. Do not debit player gold or credit NPC gold from a selector result.

At or after `BlockEndAt`, never resume. Settle NaturalExpiry (or an already authorized cancellation) using actual native earnings/refund transfers, conserving `FundedGold = ReleasedGold + RefundedGold + EscrowGold`; a terminal transition clears the suspension claim and never reactivates service. Leave the contract unavailable for new service movement while settlement is pending. Current `TrySettle` only accepts exact-boundary expiry and receipts dated that boundary. A load or time skip may first observe expiry later. Add a narrow late-expiry transition that accrues only through the fixed boundary, accepts native transfer receipts dated their *actual* completion time, and records the effective expiry separately; do not backdate a native receipt. The native earnings/refund adapter is a prerequisite to claiming end-to-end expiry/cancellation support. Until it exists, the runtime must not fabricate receipts, resume an expired contract, or report the escrow as paid out.

## Focused verification

1. Pure ledger: Active→health-owned Suspended accrues through the pause once, excludes the gap, preserves fixed expiry; only matching episode can resume, and the ordinary/unowned resume API cannot clear its claim. Counts-as-paid rest stays Active and accrues; uncovered or suspend-billing rest does not.
2. Restore/migration: version-1 Suspended becomes `LegacyUnknown`; branch/epoch-stale transition is rejected; save/reload in Seeking, Acting, Blocked and Recovered retains the owner and never resumes another suspension. Renewal-permission changes while suspended must not erase ownership.
3. Runtime: Emergency with an Active ordinary companion contract suspends before accepting a feasible owned-potion task, then one task/action moves and native healing receipt closes it. A stale contract, target, or task gate leaves service ownership unchanged. Paid rest yields movement without suspension; a later potion switches to suspension. External cure, failed treatment, blocked retry, and actor unload/reload do not duplicate movement or earnings.
4. Boundary: suspension or paid rest before fixed expiry, save/load or time skip past it, exact native payout/refund once at actual transfer time, no resume/renewal without authorization, and explicit cancellation/death settlement with conserved escrow. Native probe must inspect player/NPC gold and saved transfer receipts, not only contract status.
