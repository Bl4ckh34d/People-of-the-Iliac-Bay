# Future dialogue and English voices

## Daily plans, personality and mood — 17 October 2026

Implemented for local free-text dialogue: each addressed NPC gets independently mixed personality traits (warmth, patience, curiosity, privacy and manner), stable where the NPC has a persistent identity. Mood is recalculated from a stable daily baseline, current personal disposition, bereavement, danger response, outdoor weather, daylight, activity and whether a previous text conversation with this persistent identity is recorded. The encounter count survives saves. Rain, storms, snow, fog and sunshine supply contextual signals; darkness can make a stranger seem riskier without forcing every NPC into one response. These are guidance for free-form replies, not canned response templates.

Managed residents receive their actual ordered daily itinerary and named local stops where DFU can verify the building name. Scheduled inter-settlement trips take precedence over the home routine. Stationary native NPCs receive verified work and home obligations when available. An NPC may mention a real next destination when inclined, or keep private plans to themselves. Unmanaged vanilla wanderers and stationary NPCs without a modeled itinerary have personality and mood but no invented detailed schedule. Future work: give every persistent NPC a physical itinerary first, then expose it to dialogue; add persistent event-driven mood beyond the current day and richer acquaintance memory. Never let dialogue imply a visit that the simulation cannot perform.

### Native knowledge retrieval and map requests — implemented 17 October

Player testing exposed hallucinated temples and places in the original social-only path. That path carried identity, race, gender, role and disposition but no native answers. Prompt instructions alone did not enforce factual grounding. The installed replacement first classifies the player's intent against a bounded list of real native topics; factual replies use native answer text verbatim. Only social replies use unconstrained prose generation.

`LivingCitiesMod.ConversationKnowledge.cs` reads the active `TalkManager` trees (`ListTopicLocation`, `ListTopicPerson`, `ListTopicThings`, `ListTopicTellMeAbout`) and includes Work through its native answer path. It ranks candidates from the full trees for each question, sends up to 100 topics/8000 caption characters, and asks the local model for an intent, supplied topic ID and tone. A llama.cpp grammar constrains the format; host validation rejects invented IDs and unintended map actions. General category questions return actual child names for clarification. Unknown facts return a deterministic clarification. Only the chosen leaf calls native question/answer methods, preserving knowledge, tone, quest visibility and answer limits. Never enumerate all answer getters to construct context: they change dialogue state and can reveal locations or choose a work questor.

Explicit map requests for local buildings now check native knowledge, reaction and indoor restrictions, establish the real native subject and use the normal `%loc` discovery path. Discovery is checked before reporting success. Quest/person/regional answers retain their native reveal behavior rather than being forcibly marked. Pronoun requests such as “mark it” resolve to the previous answered subject, including an intervening native topic click; a default/previewed topic is not an answered subject. The model cannot provide arbitrary coordinates or executable commands.

### Disposition and input focus

Map intent validation also requires an explicit request rather than a mere mention of a map. A named target must match the selected real topic; a pronoun resolves to the previous answered subject. Invalid or stale follow-up subjects return clarification, including when the classifier is unavailable. Regression checks cover these cases.

The focused input sets `OverridesHotkeySequences=true`; DFU suppresses button shortcuts while it has focus and resumes them after focus leaves. A live regression failed before the change and passed afterward. The framed slot and native topic mouse controls remain in the original Talk window.

Free-text classification yields neutral, polite, rude or insult. The host applies fixed deltas of 0, +1, -2 or -8; the model cannot supply numbers. Managed resident and stationary NPC identities retain personal offsets in the additive `WorldState.ConversationAttitudes` field. Old saves without it load an empty ledger. Unmanaged vanilla wanderers have no durable identity, so their penalties last only for the current conversation. Faction reputation is untouched. Per-NPC offsets are bounded to -60..+20; daily positive gain is at most +3 and loss at most -30. The same normalized line applies once per game day, with at most 32 recorded lines per NPC/day. Direct insults are distinguished from reporting someone else's insult. Classification can still misread subtle sarcasm; these bounds limit the consequence.

`NativeConversationHooks` adjusts the native topic reaction tier after vanilla skill/personality checks: -8 personal offset lowers one tier, -24 lowers two, and +12 raises one. Free-text factual help is refused at -24 or worse. Mood also enters social-response context. `Dialogue > ConversationDisposition` and `MapRequests` allow these effects to be disabled independently.

Welcome reception uses +1, unwelcome reception -2 through the existing daily/repetition/overall limits. Personal offsets of -24 or worse reject advances regardless of the initial choice. The model receives the resulting reception as authoritative context; it must not invent a spouse or promise a relationship. Actual marriage-specific weighting remains unimplemented.

# Latest accepted dialogue rules — 17 October 2026

These thresholds supersede the earlier arbitrary one-to-three-insult ejection rule and the old personal penalty/daily penalty caps.

- First direct insult to a particular NPC costs 10 disposition; further insults cost 25 each, including repeated wording. Rudeness costs 2.
- Personal penalties can reach the engine minimum of -100 actual disposition. Positive personal gains stop at five points above the native faction/social reaction baseline. Faction reputation itself is unchanged.
- At -50 in the NPC's own home, shop or inn, the NPC demands the player leave and refuses information and map help. The context must distinguish ownership from simply being a visitor or tenant.
- At -100 the conversation ends. Outdoors, the NPC physically runs to an available guard/patrol before reporting. Indoors, the NPC immediately calls the finite garrison; guards still travel to the building and enter. The minor charge is Trespassing and must not replace an existing stronger charge.
- A valid apology can improve the personal reaction within the positive limits. The LLM receives authoritative location/ownership context and cannot invent that it owns the current building.
- At +75 actual disposition, a living resident can permit taking items from containers in their own private home. This does not authorize taking shop stock, inn rental property or public/faction property.
- Accepted pricing rule (supersedes the earlier 10%-discount decision): after vanilla pricing, actual disposition -100 raises shop purchases, repair fees and paid inn-room prices by up to 50%; 0 leaves native prices unchanged; +100 lowers them by up to 15%. Sale proceeds move in the opposite direction: -100 pays up to 50% less and +100 up to 15% more. Signed adjustments scale linearly and round down in absolute gold. Free rooms stay free; positive prices and proceeds stay at least one gold.
- The input keeps the entire draft but scrolls its visible text horizontally with the caret; neither edge may render outside the field. The generated HUD contains the recessed input area, and the text control is transparent.

Implementation and native acceptance status must be checked against the current code and test logs; this section records requirements, not a claim that every adapter is finished.
