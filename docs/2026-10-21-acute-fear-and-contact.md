# Acute danger fear and player contact — checkpoints 133–138

## Implemented behavior

World schema 41 adds one independently persisted acute-fear record per known NPC. Fresh native hostile-enemy observations maintain fear without adding it every frame. Refreshes are limited to once per 60 in-game seconds per NPC, including changes of source; a stronger baseline can take effect immediately. Source IDs describe the last observation, including session-only IDs. They are not durable assertions that a particular enemy remains alive.

Acute fear decays exponentially from its last observation. The provisional base half-life is two in-game hours, adjusted by the existing stable sensitivity, emotional-stability, self-esteem, humor and event-severity policy. The selected rate is saved. Reads and reloads do not reroll it. A weaker later observation preserves a stronger decayed residual and its slower rate while that residual dominates. Unknown/dead identities, invalid observations and backward clocks cannot write new fear. Schema 40 and older migrate to an empty acute ledger; schema 41 validates and deep-copies every record.

Player-directed fear retains its separate seven-day base recovery policy. Verified direct conversation contact projects the current decayed residual at 1.25 times its value, rounded and capped at 100. Contact never restores the historical peak, resets the saved recovery anchor, increases trust or changes resentment. General outdoor player visibility, concealment, sneak and hearing remain separate future work.

The production hostile-witness path feeds acute fear only after its existing hostile-target and direct-witness checks. The actor additionally verifies its exact store and restore epoch, living/awake/active state and quest protection. A first eligible observation creates the deterministic saved personality through the existing native faction-background resolver before applying trait-sensitive fear. Rejected writes do not create a personality.

Conversation snapshots separate stored interpersonal fear, the current contact projection and non-player danger fear with observation provenance. New observations invalidate an older in-flight context by record revision and observation time. Current TTS delivery uses the larger of acute and contact fear for generic vocal shading, without changing trust or attributing an unrelated enemy to the player. No new voice samples were generated for this checkpoint.
