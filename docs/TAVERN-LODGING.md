# Tavern lodging and travelers

## Implementation checkpoint, 15 October 2026

Schema8 adds a persisted room/booking ledger in `src/LivingCities.Core/Lodging.cs`. An accepted reservation exclusively reserves every bed in its room for a half-open check-in/check-out interval, even while the guest is away. Requests are first-come, deterministic by room ID, idempotent by booking ID and cannot overlap another booking for the room or the same guest. Player reservations receive the same occupancy protection; no reserved vacancy is invented. Non-player travelers must have a home settlement different from the tavern's. Party size cannot exceed room bed count.

Extensions fail atomically when they conflict with another accepted booking. Earliest vacancy considers requested stay duration, including future bookings and exact checkout boundaries. Unknown beds fail explicitly. Room layout changes are rejected until explicit reconciliation exists, preserving old entitlements instead of silently deleting them. Snapshots validate ownership, unique room/bed/booking IDs and overlapping intervals and deep-copy the ledger. Schemas1–7 migrate with no invented reservations.

Verification:316/316 core tests (`work/Lodging-green.log`), Unity build/native WorldState serialization (`work/Lodging-build.log`, `LIVING_CITIES_LODGING_SAVE_PASS` in the Unity build log), and actual existing-fixture save/load plus welfare/surrender regression (`work/Lodging-save-regression-pass.log`). User amendment on 14 October 2026. The initial suggestion to keep one bed free was explicitly withdrawn. Do not reserve a guaranteed player bed.

Taverns are commercial establishments, not residential households. Separate the keeper/owner, employees, local social visitors and overnight travelers. These people do not inherit one household surname merely because they share the tavern. Preserve the keeper's food, drink and rental services. Town locals may drink, eat, socialize and gather immediately outside the entrance without renting a bed. Visits increase from late afternoon through evening and early night; varied departures continue into late night, approximately 03:00. Do not place every patron on the same arrival/departure schedule.

Travelers can arrive and depart throughout the day. In walled settlements, travel across city gates must respect actual gate opening/closing: no arrival through a closed gate at night. Unwalled villages, hamlets and towns allow arrivals and departures at any hour. A closed gate constrains the route across the wall, not movement already inside town.

All routine times need small deterministic variation per NPC/day, including sleep, shopping, travel, tavern visits, walks and social visits. Variation must preserve activity constraints and survive save/load or unloading. Account for route duration: a shopper may leave before opening if arrival is at or after opening. Early arrivals wait outside until access is available. Use distinct reachable waiting positions and spacing so waiting NPCs do not overlap or obstruct the doorway. Late plans must respect closing times rather than entering a closed shop. Sleeping and waking must be consistent with the occupancy-based residential lock policy in HOUSEHOLD-LOCKS.md.

Travelers have homes outside the current settlement. They travel into town, pay for one-night or multi-day lodging, visit local destinations during their stay, and return to the road when the visit ends. Local residents may visit taverns socially, but should not be generated as ordinary overnight travelers merely to fill rooms. Keep traveler identities, destinations and progress deterministic across unloaded regions.

Paid bookings reserve a room or its allocated sleeping places for a defined interval, including when the guest is outside. Physical NPC presence alone is not the booking record. First come, first served: check availability and commit the reservation atomically so player and NPC requests cannot double-book. Do not evict an existing paid player booking to make room for simulated guests.

Rooms occupied/reserved by another guest cannot be used by the player for sleeping. Show a clear occupied-room/bed message. Exclude all beds belonging to an exclusively reserved room from other bookings; model actual room grouping rather than treating every bed in one room as a separate private room. Household bed sharing does not automatically authorize strangers sharing a paid private room.

When fully booked, the keeper refuses the new rental and reports the earliest time an eligible room becomes free, calculated from active reservation checkout times. Use the game's calendar and check requested stay duration, not merely whether a room is empty at this instant. Future extensions of stays must respect already accepted reservations. Checkout releases occupancy; cancellation, death, missing/inaccessible room and mod-layout changes need explicit reconciliation without silently erasing payment or player entitlement.

Persist traveler ID, tavern/room identity, check-in/out times, payment/booking state, party membership where applicable and current visit schedule. Save/load and time skips must not duplicate travelers or leave permanent stale reservations. Reconcile vanilla player room rental, rest markers, quest tavern NPCs and room allocation before replacing those behaviors.

Delivery remains future work within civilian schedules/nightlife and travel integration. Expose settings for traveler demand, stay lengths and lodging behavior when implemented. Test no vacancies, competing requests, day rollover, multi-day stay, guest out during paid stay, player rental, early/late checkout, unloading/reloading and earliest-vacancy dialogue.
