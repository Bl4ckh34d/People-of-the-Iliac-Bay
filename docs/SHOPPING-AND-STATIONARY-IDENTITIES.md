# Shopping behavior and stationary NPC identities

0.3.15 household bugfix replaces unclaimed generic residential flats with the authoritative saved family representation, excluding active quest resources/actions/listeners and service/faction sites. This prevents extra residents over bed capacity. It is a temporary reconciliation boundary: original appearance/identity mapping and shopkeeper service/bed integration still need implementation. Do not classify these extra generic house flats as planned visitors. See HOUSEHOLD-RECONCILIATION.md.

Crime-response clarification: a shopkeeper who becomes mobile to attack or flee cannot be talked to or traded with until the crime response is cleared and normal behavior resumes. Preserving the service role means preserving identity and later service availability, not permitting interaction during combat or flight. Apply the restriction to native activation and any new dialogue entry point, including reloads and the stationary-to-mobile handoff.

Approved requirement, 14 October 2026: a shop visit is a finite sequence of activities, not standing at one generic interior anchor. Customers walk between accessible shelves, pause facing goods, approach the shopkeeper, spend a bounded interval haggling, then leave and resume their next scheduled activity. Use physical routes, occupied-space avoidance and distinct waiting positions. Customer browsing must stay in the sales area, not private bedrooms. A missing or unreachable shelf must have a bounded fallback; it must not leave a customer stuck indefinitely. Persist or deterministically reconstruct activity phase and deadlines across interior transitions and reloads.

All stationary NPCs except main-quest NPCs must participate as real persistent people, with names, identities, household/home ownership where appropriate, beds, relationships and schedules. They can become mobile to work, travel, flee or fight. Preserve an existing native name and identity rather than inventing a duplicate person. Retain trade, guild and other service roles when the actor changes presentation. Side-quest participation remains possible: maintain quest bindings to that same person and the correct location/name rather than treating all quest participants as permanently excluded.

A shopkeeper with one bed upstairs occupies that bed; do not allocate another permanent resident over the keeper's capacity. A bedless shopkeeper lives in a separate assigned home and returns there after closing. Visitors do not acquire bed ownership from being inside a shop. Main-quest NPCs remain outside this general conversion under this requirement; their behavior needs explicit quest-specific handling.

Current implementation is incomplete: generated residents receive a 20–60 game-minute visit and stand at generic interior anchors. Native stationary keepers remain separate, with no integrated home/bed identity. In SAVE34, The Superior General Store (131343) has no generated household and has scheduled visitors from other homes; that does not yet reserve its upstairs bed for the native keeper. These requirements extend the existing stationary conversion milestone and must not be marked complete by a visitor timer alone.

Acceptance: observed shelf-to-shelf travel and facing, bounded haggling and exit, crowded counter queues, no private-bedroom browsing, missing shelf/keeper fallback, closure during a visit, unloaded/reloaded visit continuity, one-bed keeper occupancy, bedless keeper commute, preserved buying/selling dialogue, named stationary-to-mobile identity continuity, flight/fight and death, side-quest binding continuity, and explicit main-quest exclusion.
