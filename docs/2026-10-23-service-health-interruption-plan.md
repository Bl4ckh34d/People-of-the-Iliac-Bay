Paid rest can remain billable at Important priority within the existing first-treatment health threshold (50–75% health). This does not change the ordinary first-treatment threshold above 75%. Previously admitted treatment can switch to a potion only after a health-owned service pause. A new injury before physical return transfers the existing unpaid pause to the exact new care episode without restarting billing. Resume requires full health and completed physical return. ## Invariants and current seam

`NpcGoalArbiter.Select` currently defers every Active paid service contract. `StreetPopulation.HealthCare.PrepareNpcHealthCare` supplies that flag from `HasActivePaidServiceDuty` before the existing task/action mover runs. Native alarm, quest ownership, protected residents, and the guard combat exception retain their existing precedence. The ledger's `TrySuspend` accrues earned service through the pause time; `TryResume` excludes the paused interval and leaves `BlockEndAt` fixed. Neither transition records a reason. Existing native service adapter only funds reservations. A complete interruption therefore needs both durable pause ownership and an actual earnings/refund settlement adapter.

## 1. Persist a contract-owned pause

Extend `NpcServiceContractState` and the pure contract with `SuspensionOwnerKind` (`None`, `LegacyUnknown`, `HealthCare`) and `SuspensionOwnerId` (exact `NpcHealthCareRecord.Id`). Version contract DTO and `WorldState` migration together. Migrated Active contracts get `None`; migrated Suspended contracts get `LegacyUnknown` and cannot be resumed by health care. Validate that HealthCare ownership appears only on Suspended contracts, names a well-formed care episode for the same person, and survives save/reload and branch checks. Keep the owner on the contract: one `TryReplace` and one `TryCommitNpcServiceContracts` publish status, accounting, and ownership together. A separate runtime dictionary or later care-row write cannot supply this guarantee.

Add pure `TrySuspendForHealthCare(expectedRevision, now, careEpisodeId)` and `TryResumeFromHealthCare(expectedRevision, now, careEpisodeId)` transitions. Ordinary `TryResume` must reject a HealthCare-owned suspension. Store wrappers capture the existing epoch/branch/revision-bound service write ticket, check the current care episode and observed native vitals, derive one ledger replacement, and commit with empty native-transfer/authorization lists. The resume wrapper checks the exact owner, Suspended status, full observed health, no live care task, and `now < BlockEndAt`. Other suspension owners remain untouched. Reject ambiguous simultaneous Active contracts for one person until a specific contract selection rule exists.

## 2. Admit care without duplicate movement

Change arbitration from unconditional `ActiveServiceDuty` veto to source-bound service handling. A fresh paid-service interruption requires an Active contract that can be paused, a feasible fresh treatment target, and an Active/Known native recovery goal at `Urgent` or `Emergency`. A care episode already admitted under its own saved pause may continue at lower priority until fully healed; a merely created `Seeking` row does not acquire that privilege. Native alarm/quest/guard ownership remains ahead of this branch. A currently accepted care task remains with the existing task mover.

In `PrepareNpcHealthCare`, choose the actual treatment first and inspect its contract terms. For treatment requiring a pause, commit the health-owned suspension **before** cancelling the ordinary task or ending its action. If the ticket, target, contract, goal generation, or pause commit is stale, leave service movement intact. After successful suspension, re-read vitals, care revision, goal generation, and treatment target; then use existing `TryAcceptAndBindNpcHealthCareTask` and task-step dispatch. A later failure leaves a durable, non-billable health pause for retry or reconciliation; it must not silently restore billing. Continue one action executor and one mover. If native danger starts, its movement ownership wins without erasing the saved pause.

`RestTreatment` governs the chosen treatment, not the entire episode. `CountsAsPaidAvailability` with `Rest` coverage permits verified reserved-bed rest to remain Active and billable under negotiated terms; care owns movement while the contract stays Active. `SuspendBillingDuringRest` and `RestNotIncluded` require suspension before bed rest. Potion, guild, shop, or other treatment that makes the companion unavailable requires suspension irrespective of rest terms. If paid rest changes to another treatment, suspend before the new task. If an episode is already suspended, keep that pause through subsequent rest and partial healing; do not resume billing mid-episode. A failed bed reservation cannot count as paid availability.

## 3. Finish care, resume or settle

After native full-health observation, let `NpcHealthCareRecoveryHandoff.TryComplete` cancel/finish treatment and save the physical return. Reconcile the contract owner after this handoff, including on load, actor unload, and early-return paths where `PrepareNpcHealthCare` cannot run. Resume only the same saved HealthCare owner, only while `now < BlockEndAt`, and only after native danger/quest ownership has cleared. Then ordinary service movement may resume on a later schedule pass. An external cure uses the same handoff. Partial treatment, `Blocked` retry, or health above the initial interruption threshold does not end the pause.

At expiry, cancellation, or death, never resume merely to close accounting. Use `TrySettle` with actual native earnings and refund transfers and save their exact receipts atomically with contract termination; clear HealthCare ownership in that terminal transition. Preserve `FundedGold = ReleasedGold + RefundedGold + EscrowGold`. Expiry while paused cannot renew because current `TryRenew` requires Active. Current `TrySettle` permits NaturalExpiry only at exact `BlockEndAt`, while load/time skip may first observe it later. Add a narrow late-expiry transition: effective service end stays `BlockEndAt`, accrual stops there, native transfer receipts use their actual later completion time, and settlement time is stored separately. Do not backdate a native receipt. Implement trusted native payout/refund with the same exact-source and rollback discipline as funding before calling this loop complete. If transfer is unavailable, retain Suspended/expired contract and escrow for retry; never claim payment, renew, or dispatch service movement.

## 4. Tests and acceptance

Write failing focused tests before each change. Cover Urgent/Emergency service interruption, Important defer, native alarm/quest/guard priority, stale ticket/target with no task cancellation, exact owner and legacy Suspended rejection, save/reload/branch replay, paid rest versus suspended rest, paid-rest-to-potion switch, partial healing/blocked retry, external cure with saved physical return, and one movement owner. Ledger example: Active `0–43200`, Suspended `43200–57600`, Active `57600–86400` yields `72000` eligible seconds and the contract's floor-rounded earned gold. Test exact-boundary and late observed expiry, cancellation, death, native earnings/refund conservation, failed transfer retry, and one-time receipt use. Final native probe must inspect actual movement, health, currency, saved contract owner, and transfer receipts through save/reload; Core status alone is insufficient.
