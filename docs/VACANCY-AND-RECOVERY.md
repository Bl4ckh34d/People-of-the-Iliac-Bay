# Household deaths, cleanup and new occupants

User requirement, 14 October 2026. This refines gradual replacement rather than resurrecting killed NPCs.

Later same-day refinement: `CRIME-SCENES.md` governs silent single-occupant murders and guard discovery. In that case cleanup occurs when dispatched guards arrive after player departure, and guards occupy the scene for one hour. This is a specific override of the general cleanup timing below. Other occupants may fight or flee to raise an alarm, including previously stationary NPCs.

When every permanent resident of a household dies, preserve their death records and mark the dwelling vacant. Living residents who are at work or traveling still own the household; an empty room is not an extinct household. Offscreen death processing must not erase relationships or bereavement memories elsewhere.

Corpses remain initially. Normal cleanup is eligible after about one game day. If guards were informed, cleanup can occur after the player has left and a day/night period has passed. The exact informed cleanup boundary is an implementation default to document and expose where useful; use the next half-day boundary with an elapsed half-day minimum to avoid immediate cleanup near dawn/dusk. Never remove bodies visibly while the player remains in the interior. Player departure and elapsed time are separate conditions; re-entry before cleanup must not duplicate or reset bodies. Discovery/notification and criminal attribution remain separate: clearing a corpse does not make guards know an unseen killer.

Dense large cities can reoccupy a vacant dwelling after 3–7 game days, or offer it for sale through the local bank. Other settlements generally take 7–30 days. Small isolated hamlets, particularly after a population wipeout, bias toward the long end, approximately 30 days. The dense-city exception supersedes a universal seven-day minimum. Pick the outcome and delay deterministically once, persist them, and do not reroll on travel or loading. Keep homes vacant during the delay. Guards have their separate bounded recruitment process.

Replacement residents are newly created immigrants, not recycled dead residents and not existing town NPCs reassigned secretly. Give the incoming family new persistent IDs and a new household generation, respecting current family/surname and soft housing-size rules. Preserve references to the deceased generation in memories, estates and death records. A whole-town wipeout must not trigger an instant population reset or force all replacement families to arrive together.

Vacancy outcomes are mutually exclusive: occupied by a new family, listed for sale, or still vacant. Integrate bank listings with actual DFU ownership rather than displaying a fake sale option. Respect purchased homes, existing quests and protected NPCs; never sell a player-owned house or create replacements inside a quest-owned dwelling without a validated compatibility rule. When sold, cancel the migration reservation for that property. Settings changes do not silently undo deaths or active ownership.

Persist household generation, extinction time, death/corpse IDs, notification time, cleanup eligibility/completion, outcome, recovery deadline and ownership/listing state. Tests must cover save/load/time jumps, an absent surviving household member, player staying/re-entering, informed versus undiscovered deaths, dense-city 3–7 days, isolated-hamlet delay, no resurrection/ID reuse, family replacement versus sale exclusivity and player property protection.

Status: approved future implementation within population persistence, death handling and settlement recovery. Current core only persists individual alive/dead flags; corpse cleanup, immigration, household generations and bank-listing integration are not yet implemented.
