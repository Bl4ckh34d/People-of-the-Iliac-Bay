## Approved hood-layer direction (22 October)

After canonical base faces, construct compatible hood variants as three separate
layers: animated base face, semitransparent hood-shadow matte, then opaque hood
and other foreground occlusion. The user explicitly approved this reuse direction.
Keep the face editable and reuse those static layers across emotions and visemes.
Calibrate alignment and shadow strength against the derivative's original neutral
portrait before adopting a variant. A uniform gray overlay is only a starting
candidate: preserve the source's skin hue, shadow gradients and hood edge. Do not
claim pixel-exact identity from a similar face or from a gray overlay alone.
Retain the original variant and masks independently. Existing exact-mask reuse
checks remain valid for unshadowed matches; this layered shadow adaptation still
requires implementation and visual acceptance on an actual hooded pair.

`animation-policy.json` is the machine-readable exception list. Before generating a
portrait, check that list and inspect its actual original. Difficult faces get a
recorded limitation and are deferred; do not repeatedly reroll them. Add newly found
exceptions to the list with bank, record, reason and intended limited treatment.

Palette fidelity is required at native scale: compare original and composed faces
side by side before adoption. Reject unnaturally red lips, harsher contrast, black
brow strokes or bright eye flashes introduced by generation. Technical mask checks
do not establish palette fidelity. Inspect legitimate new teeth/interior pixels
separately from lip color. Do not mass-roll out a color-shifted revision.
Retain a before/after baseline when correcting colors; reject detail loss in teeth,
mouth cavities and iris highlights even when every pixel belongs to the allowed
palette. Record078's global reference-palette probes were rejected on this basis.
Its revision5 uses new neutral/angry mouth sources and the existing skin anchors;
the skin fringe is reduced, with a narrow dark/warm lip edge still pending review.
